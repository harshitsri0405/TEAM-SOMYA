  Datasets

The Urban Sensing system uses multiple datasets to train and test AI/ML models for detecting road conditions, traffic, pedestrians, and other urban-sensing events.

 1.  Pothole / Road Damage Detection
- **Dataset:** Pothole / Road Damage Dataset
- **Purpose:** Detection of potholes and damaged road surfaces.
- **Link:** [https://universe.roboflow.com/school-ewglc/pathole-detection-rmkbc/browse?queryText=&pageSize=50&startingIndex=0&browseQuery=true]

2.  Vehicle Detection & Counting
- **Dataset:** Vehicle Detection / Vehicle Counting Dataset
- **Purpose:** Vehicle detection, counting, and traffic-density estimation.
- **Link:** [https://universe.roboflow.com/yolov5-pn32l/vehicle-counting-ykrxz/browse?queryText=&pageSize=50&startingIndex=0&browseQuery=true]

 3. Traffic Sign Detection
- **Dataset:** Traffic Signs Dataset
- **Purpose:** Detection and classification of traffic signs and damaged/missing signboards.
- **Link:** [https://universe.roboflow.com/traffic-management-no13p/indian-traffic-sign-dataset]

 4.  Pedestrian Detection
- **Dataset:** Pedestrian Detection Dataset
- **Purpose:** Detection of pedestrians, especially in vulnerable/school-zone areas.
- **Link:** [https://universe.roboflow.com/btp-x2alu/pedestrian-izcag]

 5.  Zebra Crossing / Road Marking Detection
- **Dataset:** Zebra Crossing & Road Marking Dataset
- **Purpose:** Detection of zebra crossings and road markings.
- **Link:** [https://universe.roboflow.com/fruitdetection-ulcz9/zebra-crossing-8z66c/browse?queryText=&pageSize=50&startingIndex=0&browseQuery=true]

6.  Road Divider / Median Detection
- **Dataset:** Road Divider / Median Dataset
- **Purpose:** Detection of road dividers and missing/damaged dividers.
- **Link:** [https://universe.roboflow.com/01-um21c/road-road-road-fowdd]

 7.  Waterlogging Detection
- **Dataset:** Waterlogging / Flooded Road Dataset
- **Purpose:** Detection of waterlogged and flooded road areas.
- **Link:** [https://universe.roboflow.com/yolo-and-car-accident-detection-xaltb/waterlogging/browse?queryText=&pageSize=50&startingIndex=0&browseQuery=true]

 8. Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR/ALPR)
- **Dataset:** Number Plate Detection Dataset
- **Purpose:** Vehicle number plate detection and recognition for traffic monitoring and hit-and-run analysis.
- **Link:** [https://universe.roboflow.com/karthikeyini/number-plate-detection-sgw9a]

 9.  School Zone / Vulnerable Pedestrian Detection
- **Dataset:** Pedestrian / School Zone Dataset
- **Purpose:** Identification of pedestrians and vulnerable road users near school zones.
- **Link:** [https://universe.roboflow.com/btp-x2alu/pedestrian-izcag]
